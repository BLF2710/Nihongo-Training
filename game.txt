GAME IDEAS: action first, Japanese as the mechanic
====================================================

Built: Island Errands (/games/errands). A top-down village where you walk around, talk to the island cast in
Japanese and run errands. The ideas below are for later; each uses the island cast and the learner's traveller.

When Island Errands grows: each new district is a new village file in nihon-web/src/data/errands (map rows,
buildings, gates, villagers and their conversations, errands). The other games could then live inside the villages
as arcade machines or festival stalls.


1. Kana Dash (endless runner)
-----------------------------
Controls:  Space or tap to jump, down to slide; up/down switch lanes. The traveller runs across the islands.
Japanese:  Gates hang ahead, each marked with a romaji sound; only the lane with the matching kana stays open.
           Later stages swap romaji for meanings: run through 「みず」 when the sign says "water".
Game:      Speed ramps up, combo multipliers, coins buy hats for the traveller.
Scope:     Small. A canvas loop and lane logic on the existing kana data (lib/kanaDeck.ts).


2. Ink Duel (rhythm combat)
---------------------------
Controls:  Arrow keys / WASD to dodge; draw strokes with the mouse or a finger.
Japanese:  Kurama or a yokai attacks in patterns. To counter, trace a kanji's strokes in order; stroke-order
           accuracy sets the damage. Bosses demand compound words.
Game:      Dodging plus a skill-based attack, with a boss per JLPT level.
Scope:     Medium to large. Stroke recognition is the hard part; stroke data already exists
           (data/kanjiStrokes.json, data/kanaStrokes.json, KanaWritingCanvas).


3. Particle Platformer
----------------------
Controls:  Move, jump, double-jump.
Japanese:  Each platform is a word of a sentence and the gaps between them are particles. Collect は／を／に／で
           and throw them into the gaps: the right particle becomes a bridge, the wrong one crumbles.
Game:      Precise platforming plus sentence-building puzzles in the same moment.
Scope:     Medium. Reuses Particle Drop's sentence and gap finder (lib/particleDrop.ts).


4. Onsen Tycoon (management sim)
--------------------------------
Controls:  Click to place baths, lanterns and food stalls.
Japanese:  Guests arrive with requests in Japanese, like 「あついおふろがいいです」. Serving them right earns money
           and ratings. Vocabulary grows from N5 up to N1.
Game:      A management loop with upgrades that brings learners back daily; ties into the streak.
Scope:     Medium to large. Momo (the onsen monkey) runs it.


5. Yokai Typing Defense (tower defense)
---------------------------------------
Controls:  The keyboard (a Japanese IME, or romaji converted to kana as you type).
Japanese:  Yokai walk down paths toward the onsen, each carrying a word. Typing its reading fires a lantern at it.
           Towers placed along the path auto-attack yokai of one word type (verbs, adjectives…).
Game:      Tower defense with typing as the aiming; builds real typing skill.
Scope:     Small to medium. Reuses lib/romaji.ts for romaji-to-kana.
